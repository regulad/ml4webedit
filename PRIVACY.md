# Dream Team Hard Mode Toggle privacy policy

*Effective 26 November 2026. Publisher: Parker Wahle, regulad@regulad.xyz.*

The Dream Team Hard Mode Toggle is a web page that turns Hard Mode on or off in a *Mario & Luigi: Dream Team* save file (README.md).

## Your save stays in your browser

The page reads the save you choose, changes it and hands it back to you, all in your browser. It doesn't upload the save, or send its name, its contents, its slot or whether Hard Mode is on, anywhere.

## Diagnostics

To find and fix problems, and to count how often the page is used, the site sends crash reports and usage counts to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io), a service that stores them on our behalf, in the United States. A copy of the page built from its source code, or a preview of a change, sends nothing.

- **Crash reports:** the error, and where in the page's code it happened.
- **Usage counts:** one record for each visit to the page, and whether it ended in an error; and one for each edited save you download, with how long it took.
- **Your browser:** its name and version, your operating system and its version, the page's address, and the page's version.

Each visit gets a random ID, made when the page loads and kept only for that visit, so that its reports can be told apart from another's. The page doesn't send your save or anything in it, its file name, cookies, or anything else that identifies you, and our diagnostics service is set not to store IP addresses. It does note roughly where each report came from (the city, region and country), which it works out from the connection before discarding the address.

This is used only to diagnose and improve the page. It isn't sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other data. Sentry keeps it for up to 90 days.

## Turning diagnostics off

Turn on your browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control setting, and the page sends nothing from the next time it loads. A content blocker that blocks sentry.io has the same effect.

## Children

The page isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect children's personal information.
